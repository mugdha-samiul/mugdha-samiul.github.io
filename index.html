I am a Lecturer in the Department of Software Engineering at Daffodil International University (DIU), Dhaka. I received my B.Sc. in Software Engineering from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST) in 2025. I am also a Research Assistant (remote) at Concordia University, supervised by Prof. Yuhong Yan.

My research interests center on AI for Software Engineering, LLM-based Test Generation, Software Testing and Debugging, and Trustworthy AI Evaluation — using program analysis and mutation testing to make LLM-assisted software testing more effective and efficient.
