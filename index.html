I am a Lecturer in the Department of Software Engineering at Daffodil International University (DIU), Dhaka. I received my B.Sc. in Software Engineering from Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST) in 2025. I am also a Research Assistant (remote) at Concordia University, supervised by Prof. Yuhong Yana.

My research interests lie at the intersection of AI for Software Engineering and Trustworthy AI. I study LLM-based software testing and debugging, using program analysis and mutation testing to improve AI-assisted software engineering, while also exploring LLM evaluation, AI reliability, and human-centered AI systems to understand how AI can be made more trustworthy and effective in real-world applications.
